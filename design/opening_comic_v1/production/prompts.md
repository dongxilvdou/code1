# 正式游戏插画生成记录

模式：内置 image_gen，参考图编辑/生成。用户最终选择彩色样页。所有成品已复制到 assets/cinematics/comic，无临时目录依赖。

01_awake：以用户最终附图为编辑目标，移除全部黑色文字框和文字，补回背景；保持三格、猫、布局、颜色、场景。保留黑色分格线，不增加文字。

## 02_memory

```text
Create PAGE TWO of an original 16:9 anime comic, using the reference as the exact identity/style anchor. Same slim adult orange-and-white tabby, white muzzle/blaze/chest/paws, olive-green slit pupils, pink nose, no collar; simple black outlines and sparse flat cel colors, not dense fur. Same warm wooden ordinary villa.
TWO wide panels only, separated by a thick black straight diagonal gutter from left edge at 52% image height to right edge at 44% height.
TOP: cat-eye-height view into the enormous quiet living room: high coffee table legs, cream sofa, white walls, dark wood doorframes. The small back of the cat's head/ears is at lower right foreground. Quiet cool-gray shadows with a little warm light. Upper-left 40% remains a simple darker doorway/wall, to hold future game text (do not draw text).
BOTTOM: warm affectionate memory. Same cat, eyes closed and content, head and chest fill the RIGHT 60%. One anatomically correct woman's hand in a plain beige sleeve gently strokes its forehead from above right. No owner face/body; no lab coat or hospital. Left 38% is a subdued simple wall with space for future text. Warm amber/cream, restrained light. Pose must be gentle and feline.
IMPORTANT no lettering, captions, speech balloons, labels, watermark, or UI anywhere. No extra cat, no human face, no extra limbs. Match supplied colored reference, not black-and-white. Artwork for direct game use, polished composition.
```

## 03_owner

```text
Create PAGE THREE of an original anime comic, 16:9 landscape, same precise sparse-line flat-colored orange-white tabby and richer painted home interiors as both references. Reference 1 establishes identity and reference 2 establishes the gentle owner's beige sleeve.
TWO wide horizontal panels, ONE thick black diagonal straight gutter from left edge at 52% height to right edge at 44% height. No other panels.
TOP: affectionate domestic memory of owner coming home. At cat-eye height, open wooden entrance door in the background, only woman's lower legs in plain dark trousers and slippers visible in center-right (face and upper body outside frame). The SAME slender orange-and-white cat at lower right welcomes her, tail raised with gently curled tip, brushing its head affectionately against her lowered hand. Correct four-legged cat anatomy, no collar, white chest and paws, olive eyes, pink nose, same markings. Warm amber, calm cream walls and striped terracotta mat. Leave left upper 38% as quiet dark wood wall for future text; draw no text.
BOTTOM: memory of owner ill at home, no gruesome material. Close quiet scene of bedside, a plain drinking glass of water, tissue box and ONE small unlabelled medicine bottle. Only owner's relaxed slender hand/wrist resting weakly on the blanket from right; beige sleeve identical to reference 2, no lab coat, no needles, no blood, no human face. The cat rests its white muzzle/forehead gently beside her hand in the RIGHT 55% of frame. Soft cream blanket folds. The left 38% is a darker plain bedside/background area to hold future game text. Make the hand anatomically credible, five fingers relaxed. Gentle sadness, less saturated warm light, no dramatic gleaming eyes, no photoreal fur.
Cat drawn with light black contour, simple broad orange/brown tabby markings, white muzzle and bib, minimal strokes, hard-edged cel shadows only. Match selected colored style, not grayscale.
CRITICAL NO text, lettering, speech bubbles, caption boxes, logos or watermark; no labels on medication. No extra cat, extra hand or malformed limbs. This is final comic art for editable game captions added later.
```

## 04_resolve

```text
Create PAGE FOUR, the concluding page of an original colored anime comic, landscape 16:9. Same individual cat and same art style as reference: slender adult orange-white tabby with large white bib, white muzzle blaze and paws, ringed orange tail, olive-green slit pupils and pink nose, no collar. Economical black ink outlines, simple flat colors and sparse hard-edged shading for cat, richer softly painted warm wood/ivory house behind it. NO detailed realistic fur, NO black-and-white.
TWO wide horizontal panels only, one thick black straight diagonal gutter from left edge at 52% height to right edge at 44% height.
TOP: we have returned from a memory to the present. Three-quarter close-up of the SAME cat in the RIGHT 60% of frame at its eye level. It has raised its head with quiet determination, ears attentive, mouth closed, not angry or human-like. One front white paw rests visibly on the striped terracotta mat at the bottom edge. Background: entrance hallway, dark brown doorframe, ivory walls. LEFT 38% mostly dark wall/door to leave clear space for editable captions. Lighting slightly subdued compared with affectionate memory.
BOTTOM: a first-person cat view moving ahead into the same hallway: TWO white forepaws, one taking a small natural step from the lower-right edge onto the warm wooden floor just beyond the terracotta-and-cream mat. At lower center-right on the floor sits a SINGLE SMALL FOLDED BLANK PAPER NOTE with no writing, within paw reach. Doorframes and tall furniture legs recede in perspective toward right/back. LEFT 40% of lower panel is quiet dark floor/wall with no important object, for future text and game title. Cat-scale composition; keep claws retracted, anatomically correct small round paws and no duplicate limbs.
Match the supplied selected colored page closely, including muted ochre orange fur, green eyes, warm light and black slanted gutters. No human in this page, no lab, no reveal of later story.
ABSOLUTELY NO text, title, writing, caption boxes, balloons, letters, logos, labels or watermark anywhere. The game's UI will add all text and title. All spaces requested are natural background, not empty white rectangles.
```
